Every export leaves something behind: a space at the end of an order id, a phone number in four pieces, a total that doesn't multiply out. Sheetwise marks them, sets the harmless ones aside — and stops where a person has to look. 每张导出的表都会剩下点什么：订单号末尾多一个空格，电话被拆成四段，金额和数量乘单价对不上。Sheetwise 把它们标出来，把不用问的收好——然后停在需要人看一眼的地方。
